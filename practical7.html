Student Support

Department Help Desk

Quick answers to frequently asked academic & administrative questions.

Log in to the Student ERP portal with your roll number. Go to Academics > Course Registration, choose your core and elective subjects, and click submit before the published deadline.

A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory in each registered course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examinations.

Submit an online request under the Certificates & Requests section of your portal. Once approved by the department head, you can download the digitally signed copy within 2 working days.

Your mentor's name, official email, and visiting hours are listed on your Student Profile Dashboard and the department notice board.
